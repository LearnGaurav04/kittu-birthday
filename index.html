Chapter 06 · A letter for you

Dear Kittu,

Happy birthday, you. ❤️

Six months ago, I couldn't have known how much you would come to mean to me.

From that night at Brahma Brews to the random drives, the concert, the laughs, the stupid moments, and all the little things in between — I'm really grateful that all of those moments happened with you.

I love that you're a mix of everything. You can be the cutest person in the world, completely chaotic five minutes later, ridiculously caring, and still somehow have the heart of a kid who gets genuinely excited about bhujia.

And then there are your eyes. I don't think you realise how beautiful they are.

You've made these six months really special, Kittu. I hope you know how loved, appreciated and special you are — today and on all the ordinary days too.

Here's to everything we've already experienced, and to all the memories we haven't made yet.

Happy birthday, Kittu. ❤️

Love,
Golu
